URANIUM — how to use it
=====================

WHAT YOU NEED
- Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3, running with Fabric Loader and Fabric API.
  (One file works on all three. Older versions won't work.)
- Nothing else: Uranium.exe brings its own Java.

GETTING STARTED
1. Start Minecraft and wait for the main menu.
2. Unzip Uranium.zip anywhere and double-click Uranium.exe.
3. Click the big Inject button (it lights up once it sees Minecraft).
   Advanced lists every Java program if you need to pick one by hand.
4. In the game, press Numpad 8 to open the menu.
   - Left-click a module to turn it on or off.
   - Right-click a module to see its settings.

IMPORTANT: inject only ONCE each time you start Minecraft. Injecting again
without restarting the game loads a second copy, and the two will fight
each other (double hits, lag). Restart Minecraft first, then inject.

To turn everything off, click Unload in the menu or in the Uranium window.
Your settings are saved automatically.

UPDATES
Every time Uranium opens it checks GitHub for a newer version. When there
is one, the Update tab gets a dot: open it to read what's new, click
Download update, then Restart Uranium. The new version goes in the builds
folder next to Uranium.exe; nothing else changes. A news line from us can
show above the Inject button too.

BUILDER
Builder.exe, next to Uranium.exe, looks after Uranium for you:
- Updates: the version you have, the newest one on GitHub with what's new,
  and Download & install. Open Uranium.exe afterwards to use it.
- Checks your setup: Minecraft 26.1.2, Fabric Loader and Fabric API
  installed, and whether Minecraft is running. A red dot says what's
  missing.
- Clean old builds: deletes downloaded versions you don't use any more.
- Reset settings: every module back to default (close Minecraft first;
  your old settings are kept as spear-injector.properties.old).
- Reset my hand: deletes humans.txt, back to an average hand.
- Open logs, Open config, Open Uranium folder.


WHAT EACH MODULE DOES
--------------------

COMBAT
  Spear Dash      Swaps to your spear, lunges, and swaps back, on one key.
  Auto Stun       Axe through their shield, then a mace hit while it's down.
                  Reaches as far as the weapon in your hand (4.5 with a spear).
  Play Style      How the aim and timing of Cart, Aim Assist, Kill Aura
                  and Trigger Bot feel.
                    Mouse test: the first time you open the menu, dots pop
                    up and you click them. Uranium reads how your hand
                    moves the mouse (where your push stops before you
                    correct, how often you go past, where you're fastest,
                    how much the path bends) and its aim moves the same
                    way. Only the shape: being slow or missing doesn't
                    matter and isn't copied. Take it again from here, or
                    go back to an average hand. Your hand is saved in
                    humans.txt in the Uranium folder (next to Uranium.bat),
                    in plain words: keep it, share it, or delete it to go
                    back to an average hand.
                    Calm (off): smooth even turns, steady timing. Quietest.
                    Cracked (on): plays like someone sweating a fight.
                    Turns are quick and hard and go a hair past before
                    snapping back. Waits are shorter and uneven: mostly
                    quick, now and then a little late. And it heats up:
                    every hit you land or take and every cart you shoot
                    makes the next move faster. It cools off about 6
                    seconds after the fight. Anticheats notice Cracked
                    more than Calm, so use Calm on strict servers.
  Trigger Bot     Hits whatever you're looking at, but only when the hit will
                  actually count. Settings: Chance, and Random delay: each
                  hit waits a new random time first (100 ms, then 142.1,
                  then ...) inside the Delay range, so no two hits come at
                  the same moment. Slams never wait (you'd land first).
                  Auto: Hit only works while you look like you're
                  fighting: a player is in front of you (close, near your
                  crosshair) and in the last 2.5 seconds you jumped,
                  switched hotbar slots, swung, got hit by a player or
                  sprinted at them. Holding a wind charge turns it off.
  Knockback Displacement
                  Your sprint hit sends them where you want. The extra push of
                  a sprint hit goes the way your camera faces, so when you
                  sprint-hit a player it checks every way your crosshair can
                  still be on them for lava, fire, a cobweb, a cactus, your
                  TNT cart or a drop of 3+ blocks, turns your view that way
                  (a small mouse-like turn) and sends your hit a tick later,
                  once the server has your new view. Nothing to push them
                  into: your hit goes out normally. Max turn: how far it may
                  turn (it never turns off their hitbox anyway).
                  Push: Hazards, Toward you, or Both (hazards first).
                  Toward you is for cornering a player who strafes: no hit can
                  pull them in, but the sideways push can cancel their run, so
                  they land in your path and close enough for the next hit.
  Hit Select      A slam you click while they're still immune from the last
                  hit would mostly be wasted. Hit Select holds that click and
                  sends it the moment their immunity ends.
  Slam Follow-up  Slam, then sword: the moment their immunity from your slam
                  ends, it pulls out your sword, hits, and goes back.
  Target Strafe   While you fall into a slam, holds W A S D toward the player
                  below you so you land on them, not past them. Your own keys
                  always win.
  Kill Aura       Turns your view onto the player in
                  front of you and hits when the hit counts, only once your
                  crosshair is really on them, after a human reaction delay.
                  Settings: Range, Rotation speed, FOV, Reaction, Chance,
                  Mobs too.
  Aim Assist      Hold Left Alt (change it, or pick Toggle / Always, in the
                  menu) and your aim turns onto the player nearest your
                  crosshair, with any item in your hand. Aim mode:
                    Simple  speeds up, then eases onto them. Speed is a
                            range: each target gets its own top speed.
                    Wind    copies Doomsday's aim, measured from a
                            recording: a small nudge, then one flick
                            nearly all the way (Snap), then it lets go
                            for a moment. A step every game tick (Step
                            every). The sharpest mode, and the easiest
                            for an anticheat to spot.
                    Human   reacts like a person: aims where they were a
                            moment ago (Reaction), flicks a little past on
                            big turns and comes back (Overshoot).
                  It sticks to one player until another is clearly nearer
                  your crosshair. Prediction aims ahead of a moving player
                  (0% is smoothest). Vertical aim off turns you only left
                  and right. Also: Range, FOV, Aim at (Auto / Head /
                  Center / Feet), Ignore mouse input, Check line of sight,
                  Aim while blocking, Ignore invisible, Ignore creative.
                  Off on the ground unless Ground too is on.
                  While it's on, your spear's hotbar slot glows gold.
  Mace Swap       Pulls out your mace for slams from 4.5+ blocks. Smaller
                  drops and normal hits keep your weapon, which hits harder.
  Elytra Mace     Dive at a player with an elytra on and look at them: your
                  elytra comes off by itself as you close in (a chestplate
                  from your hotbar goes on), so your mace hit counts as a
                  slam. Only when you're above them and diving. Glide again
                  puts the elytra back on after the hit if you're still in
                  the air.
  Auto Totem      Keeps a totem in your offhand.
  Auto Weapon     Switches to your best weapon when you hit holding a tool.
  Reach Swap      Uses your spear's longer reach to hit players further away.
  Quick Throw     Throws a pearl or wind charge on one key.
  Pearl Catch     Throws your pearl and sends a wind charge to meet it in the
                  air, so you teleport up. Works on a key or by right-clicking
                  a pearl.
  Elytra Swap     Swaps between elytra and chestplate on one key.

CART (TNT minecarts)
  High Tier       Right-click a block with a rail: it puts the rail down,
                  puts a TNT minecart on it, lights the block your shot will
                  fly through (beside the rail or on its corner) with your
                  flint and steel, then pulls out your crossbow. Needs a rail and a TNT minecart in your hotbar;
                  flint and steel is optional.
                    Shoot: fixes your aim with a small flick so the arrow
                    goes through the fire into the cart, then fires your
                    loaded crossbow for you.
                    Off, the shot is yours (load it with a fire arrow, or
                    use a Flame crossbow).
                    Delay: how long it waits before the cart (and before
                    the shot).
                  Can't light fire there (you shoot up at the cart from under
                  it, or down at it, or you have no flint and steel)? With a
                  lava bucket in your hotbar it turns your aim onto the spot,
                  pours lava where the shot will pass, and turns back onto
                  the cart. An arrow through lava catches fire too. The lava
                  stays there after, so watch your step.
                  No flint and steel and no lava at all: the rail and cart
                  go down about twice as fast, with a small hand shake on
                  each click instead of a smooth move.
                  The faster you move (an elytra dive), the shorter every
                  wait gets, so the cart still goes down before you're past
                  it. Out of reach, it stops instead of trying anyway.
                  No way to light it at all? It pulls out your
                  bow instead, a Flame one first, and with Shoot on draws it
                  and aims a little high for the arrow's drop.
                  While it's on, any crossbow in your hotbar that isn't
                  loaded gets a big red cover, so you load it in time (with
                  Armor Slots' alerts on, the !!! over it does that instead).
                    Chance: how often it acts (100% every time).
                    Macro mode (same switch in High Tier and Low Tier,
                    also used by Block): the module presses your own
                    keys, your hotbar number key and your right-click,
                    and the game itself does the switch and the click on
                    its next tick, just like your fingers. Nothing gets
                    sent by the module. Each press takes a game tick, so
                    it is a bit slower. In High Tier your own right-click
                    goes to the game as a real click: it puts the rail
                    down (or, on a rail that's already there, just
                    clicks it) and the module carries on from there with
                    the cart, fire and shot.
                    Overload: two TNT carts on the same rail, one tick
                    apart, for a blast that goes through a single block
                    (it takes 2 End Stone to stop). Not in Macro mode:
                    the game's own click lands on the first cart.
                    Look: Stutter turns in quick jumps, Smooth in one even
                    turn. Either way it turns to the closest good spot on
                    the rail or fire block, so the turn is short.
                    The fire goes in any block the shot flies through on
                    its way to the cart, not only next to the rail.
                    Lava: after the blast, look back at the lava and it's
                    picked up: the empty bucket comes out the moment your
                    crosshair is on it, scoops it, and your hand goes back
                    to what you held. Your view isn't moved for it. Look
                    back within 10 seconds, or it's left there.
                    Self guard: keeps your own blast off you. Block: right
                    after the cart it puts a block (End Stone first) next
                    to the cart on your side, jumps, and shoots over it;
                    the block takes the blast for your whole body. Only
                    under Guard under (hearts, 6 to start), or whenever the
                    blast would surely pop you whatever your health (it
                    counts the armor you really wear: an elytra instead of
                    a chestplate about doubles the blast). Needs
                    a block and room for it. Block + refuse: also no
                    cart where the blast would still kill you or pop your
                    totem. Off: nothing. After each shot the log says how
                    much the blast really did to you.
                    Too steep: when no shot can reach the cart from where you
                    stand or from a jump (a wall, the guard block, the cart
                    up high), it jumps and puts a block under your feet,
                    lands on it, and jumps again to shoot from up there.
                    If the cart didn't blow (the arrow missed or went out),
                    it shoots once more with another loaded crossbow or the
                    bow, lighting the fire again first if it's gone.
                    Moved too far from the spot before the rail or cart went
                    down? The rail and cart go about 3 blocks in front of you
                    instead (the same way), and the shot follows from there.
                    Go-ping (always on): it picks up wherever you are.
                    Right-click a rail holding a TNT cart and it carries on
                    from the cart. Right-click near a TNT cart already on a
                    rail holding flint and steel or lava (say you put it
                    down, went out of reach and came back) and it carries
                    on from the fire, then the shot. Missing something? It
                    skips it like always: no flint uses lava or nothing, no
                    loaded crossbow uses the bow.
                    Hard switch: with two crossbows in your hotbar, the
                    shot always takes the loaded one, and Auto reload
                    leaves the empty one alone until both are empty (then
                    it loads them). Keeps your loaded crossbow ready instead
                    of stopping to reload mid-fight.
                    A loaded crossbow in your offhand counts: it fires from
                    there with no swap.
                    Auto reload: when a crossbow in your hotbar is empty and
                    nothing is going on, it switches to it, loads it and
                    switches back. Pick a slot yourself and it stops.
                    Replenish: after your cart blows, if your rails, TNT
                    carts or flint and steel ran out of your hotbar, it
                    opens your inventory,
                    moves the mouse to a stack (a different path each
                    time), presses the number key of the slot it ran out
                    of, so it lands in that exact slot (any junk there goes
                    back into the inventory), then closes
                    the inventory.
  Block           A TNT cart that isn't yours comes within Range: it puts
                  blocks between you and it (End Stone first, it takes
                  blasts well, then obsidian, then any full block). It picks
                  the spots that hide the most of you from the blast (a cart
                  coming in on the diagonal gets a block on the diagonal,
                  right in its line, not two beside it with a crack
                  between), takes the block out, turns your view smoothly
                  onto where it goes, places it and switches back. Blocks: how many (1-4). Your own
                  carts from High Tier and Low Tier are left alone.
                    Jump block: jump with a cart within Range and a block
                    goes under your feet too, with a calm look down and
                    back up.
                    Alert: while a cart that isn't yours is within Alert
                    range, red !!! hop at the right middle of your screen
                    with a tan arrow under them pointing to it, and how
                    many blocks away it is.
  Low Tier        Fire your bow and it works out where the arrow will come
                  down, then puts a rail and a TNT minecart there before it
                  lands. Your camera turns there in one smooth flick, like
                  your own hand, and it doesn't snap back. You can only
                  place within your normal reach, so shoot up high and let
                  it come down near you. Use a Flame bow or fire arrows so
                  the cart goes off.
                    Crossbow too: works for crossbow shots as well.
                    Look: how your view turns onto the rail spot. Flick is
                    a hand's quick flick with a small correction after;
                    Smooth is one even turn; Stutter is quick jumps. Smooth
                    and Stutter end right on the spot.
                    Delay: how long it waits between the rail and the cart.
                    Replenish: the same refill as High Tier, for rails and
                    TNT carts.
                    Auto reload: the same as High Tier's.
                    Chance: how often it acts (100% every time).
                    Lowest draw: your bow lets go by itself the moment it is
                    drawn enough to shoot (4 ticks), so the arrow is slow and
                    comes down close to you. One shot a click: hold right-click
                    and it won't draw again until you let go.
                  A flat (low angle) shot lands sooner, so the turns and the
                  wait get quicker to fit it. A crossbow in your offhand
                  counts too, but its arrow only blows a cart if it flies
                  through fire or lava on the way.

UHC
  Web Escape      Gets you out of cobwebs and fire with water.
  Auto Web        Webs the player you're looking at, on one key.
  Wind Clutch     Throws a wind charge under you right before a fall that
                  would leave you near death, so you take no fall damage.
  MLG Water       Places water under you before a hard landing.
  W-Tap           Resets your sprint after every hit (more knockback).
  Jump Reset      Jumps right as knockback hits you (less knockback).
  Auto Gapple     Eats a golden apple when your health gets low.

UTILITY
  Auto Tool       Picks the fastest tool while you mine.
  Auto Sprint     Sprints whenever you walk forward.
  Auto Walk       Walks forward for you.
  Auto Eat        Eats when you get hungry.
  Auto Fish       Reels in when a fish bites and casts again.
  Fullbright      Lights up caves and nights.
  Zoom            Hold a key to zoom in. Zoom in: Smooth (glides in and
                  out, the default) or Snap.
  Clear Weather   No rain or thunder on your screen.
  Auto Respawn    Skips the death screen.
  Hit Sound       A ding when your hit lands.
  Middle Click Friend   Middle-click a player to add them as a friend.
                  Friends are never hit or targeted.
  Debug           Hidden: type "debug" in the menu search to find it. While
                  it's on, a badge in the top left shows Human % for the
                  last minute. Its screen lists every mouse move the
                  modules made (click one to see its path and speed) and
                  everything that could get you flagged: snaps, moves at
                  an even speed, the same timing again and again, landing
                  dead on every time, two slot switches or two clicks in
                  one tick, turning far and clicking in the same tick.
                  Test buttons make a flick, stutter, glide, shake or a
                  Replenish cursor trip right there, so you can look at
                  them without a fight. Play with it on to check Cart,
                  Replenish and the rest.

HUD
  Speed HUD       A speed ring above your hotbar.
  Radar           Shows nearby players around your crosshair.
  Landing         Shows the fall damage you'll take before you land.
  Combat stats    Your ping, fall, shield timing and shield break results.
  Slam Meter      While you fall with a mace, shows under your crosshair how
                  hard the slam will hit them after their armor, and KILL or
                  POP when it's enough.
  2AM             A quiet HUD for late sessions. Out of a fight it fades
                  almost to nothing; it comes back the moment you hit, get
                  hit or draw a bow. Steel, cream and amber instead of bright
                  green, and it dims itself after midnight. It has:
                  - a thin record ring round the crosshair that fills with
                    your crossbow's charge or your attack charge,
                  - a line in the corner like "carts 7 · rails 12 · xbow
                    loaded" that shows when a count changes, then fades,
                  - dots over players for every totem they popped: amber
                    this life, grey for lives before.
                  Only one alert shows at a time.
  Armor Slots     Your four armor pieces in hotbar-style slots on the left of
                  your hotbar (past the offhand slot), with durability bars.
                    Alerts (on by default):
                    three red !!! hop over anything that needs you: armor
                    under 20% or a missing piece, a hotbar item under 10%,
                    a crossbow that isn't loaded, 2 or fewer carts, pearls or
                    golden apples, 8 or fewer rails, or an empty offhand while
                    you carry a totem.
  Pop Chams       When anyone pops a totem (you too), a see-through ghost of
                  them stays where it happened and floats up as it fades,
                  with "Name popped 3" over it: red for them, green for you.
                  The middle of your screen says who popped too.
  Damage Particles  Old 1.8.9 style: every hit on a player throws a shower of
                  red -1s off them, one per point of damage, for about 2
                  seconds. Combos make a lot of them.
  Punish Alerts   Shows when a nearby player is open: EATING (with the time
                  left), POPPED a totem, or SHIELD DOWN after your Shield
                  Break (with the seconds until they can block). Optional
                  ping sound.
  Wheels          A wheel floating above players' heads that turns one notch
                  every time they get hit. Choose who gets one and whether it
                  replaces their helmet. Only you can see it.
  Hide other clients   Hides the module lists other clients draw on screen.


GOOD TO KNOW
- No Fabric API? Everything still works except the HUD tab.
- On 26.2 and 26.3: install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for that version.
  Reach Swap and Wheels only work on 26.1.2 (they switch themselves off).
- Something didn't work? Every mace hit, dash, shield break and pearl catch
  writes a line in .minecraft\logs\spear-injector.log explaining what
  happened.
