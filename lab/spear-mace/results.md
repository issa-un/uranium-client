## What each idea is

- **Stun slam Density V double charge** (Shield): Two charges, Density V, axe first.
- **Density V pearl 20** (Mace enchant): Big pearl drop with Density V.
- **Elytra Mace + Density V (Lumen)** (Elytra): Elytra Mace from 20 with a Density V mace.
- **Pick Density or Breach by drop** (Mace enchant): Density for drops over 8, Breach under (Mace Swap style).
- **Wind charge every cooldown** (Mace chain): Re-launch with a wind charge after each slam (0.5 s charge cooldown).
- **Wind Burst III chain x3** (Mace chain): Each slam bounces you back up; three in a row.
- **Wind Burst I chain x3** (Mace chain): Same chain, weaker bounce.
- **Density V wind slam** (Mace enchant): Wind charge slam with Density V.
- **Stun slam Breach IV** (Shield): Stun slam with a Breach IV mace.
- **Breach vs netherite only** (Mace enchant): Breach only into netherite, Density otherwise.
- **Breach IV wind slam** (Mace enchant): Wind charge slam with Breach IV.
- **Two slams spaced 10 ticks** (Mace chain): Wait out the immunity between slams.
- **Stun slam from pearl 20** (Shield): Pearl high, stun slam on the way down.
- **Breach IV pearl 20** (Mace enchant): Big pearl drop with Breach IV.
- **Elytra Mace, glide again x2 (Lumen)** (Elytra): Hit, glide again, climb, second dive.
- **Elytra Mace dive 30 (Lumen)** (Elytra): Same from 30 up: a much bigger slam, faster fall.
- **Slam when they eat** (Timing): Wait for a gapple: they stand still for 1.6 s.
- **Stun slam (axe + mace same tick)** (Shield): Axe takes the shield down, mace lands the same tick.
- **Triple wind charge slam** (Mace height): Three chained charges for a huge drop.
- **Slam while they block** (Timing): Slam right as their shield is still rising (5 ticks).
- **Axe first, slam next fall** (Shield): Axe the shield on the ground, then wind charge slam.
- **Pearl up 20 slam** (Mace height): Pearl 20 up: big drop, long time to read.
- **Spear knock up, mace follow** (Hybrid): ASSUMED: spear hit knocks them up a little; slam as they fall.
- **Slam after their pop** (Timing): Slam right after a totem pop: they have 1 hp + absorption.
- **Slam then sword** (Mace chain): Slam, then a sword hit once the immunity is over.
- **Corner them, then slam** (Timing): Trap them against a wall first: no strafe space.
- **Double wind charge slam** (Mace height): Second wind charge at the top, then slam.
- **Pearl catch slam** (Movement): Pearl + wind charge catch for height anywhere.
- **Spear poke then slam** (Hybrid): Poke to start, wind charge up while they close in, slam.
- **Slam when they pearl** (Timing): Slam where their pearl lands.
- **Slam then spear poke** (Hybrid): Slam, back off, spear them as they chase.
- **Cliff drop 12** (Mace height): Fight under a ledge, drop 12 onto them.
- **Elytra cancel 30** (Mace height): Glide in high, stop gliding over them, fall 30.
- **Elytra Mace dive 15 (Lumen)** (Elytra): Lumen's Elytra Mace: dive from 15 up, elytra drops just before reach, slam.
- **Sword then slam in immunity** (Mace chain): Sword hit, then a slam inside the 10-tick immunity (only the extra counts).
- **Diagonal wind launch** (Movement): Wind charge at an angle to land on a runner.
- **Lunge escape, slam back** (Hybrid): Lunge away when low, turn and slam when they follow.
- **Pearl up 10 slam** (Mace height): Pearl onto something 10 up, drop on them.
- **Lunge then slam** (Hybrid): Wind charge up, Lunge across the gap in the air, slam.
- **Slam with high ping** (Timing): Same slam with 200 ms ping.
- **Strafe-in slam** (Movement): Steer in the air the whole fall.
- **Slam from behind** (Timing): Come down behind them: slower to react.
- **Wind charge slam** (Mace height): Wind charge at your feet, mace on the way down.
- **Slam with low ping** (Timing): Same slam with 20 ms ping.
- **Pillar 5 drop** (Mace height): Drop from a 5-block pillar next to them.
- **No-steer slam** (Movement): Straight up, straight down.
- **Elytra spear poke** (Elytra): Fly past them with a spear: the flight speed is the charge.
- **Ender pearl over, drop** (Movement): Pearl over their head and fall straight on them.
- **Lunge spear stun** (Spear shield): Lunge III in, axe + spear the same tick: shield down plus the Lunge's kinetic hit.
- **Spear stun poke at 4.5** (Spear shield): Axe + spear jab in the same tick from 4.5: the server lets the axe reach that far, so the shield drops and the jab lands (Lumen's Shield Break with a spear).
- **Spear poke at 3 blocks** (Spear): Poke close in, where aim is easier but their sword reaches you.
- **Half cooldown slam** (Timing): Slam before your mace cooldown is full (50%).
- **Spear jab poke** (Spear): Stand at 4.5 and poke, out of their sword reach.
- **Lunge III jab** (Spear): Lunge III: much faster close, bigger kinetic hit.
- **Horse charge** (Spear): Ride a horse into them (14 blocks/s).
- **Plain slam into shield** (Shield): Slam a shielding player with no axe.
- **Wind charge forward + spear** (Spear): Wind charge behind you for speed, then jab.
- **Poke when they swing** (Spear shield): Wait at 4.5: they drop the shield to swing at you, and the jab lands in that gap.
- **Spear then sword** (Spear): Poke at 4.5, step in, sword after the immunity.
- **Spear stun (axe + spear)** (Spear): Axe the shield, spear jab the same tick (axe needs 3 blocks).
- **Spear sprint charge** (Spear): Sprint at them and let the speed do damage.
- **Lunge behind, poke the back** (Spear shield): Lunge III past them and poke their back before they turn their shield around.
- **Lunge I jab** (Spear): Lunge I to close the gap, jab.
- **Spam slam** (Timing): Slam as soon as you land, no waiting (cooldown ~30%).
- **Jump slam** (Mace height): Jump and hit on the way down: barely a smash.
- **Spear poke into shield** (Spear): Poke someone holding a shield up.
